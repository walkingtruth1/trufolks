# TruFolks logo kit

Colours are the locked TruFolks Core palette (see `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` in the repo root). No green or yellow anywhere.

| Colour | Hex | Role in the logo |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | #0F2A44 | Tile, wordmark on light backgrounds |
| Deep Navy | #0A1F33 | Dark backgrounds |
| Royal Blue | #2563EB | The "F" |
| Warm Cream | #F2F1E9 | Light backgrounds, reversed tile and wordmark |
| White | #FFFFFF | Top bar of the "T" on the Navy tile |

**Font:** Inter ExtraBold (800), letter-spacing about -2.75%. It's free on Google Fonts. The wordmark in the SVGs is live text, so install Inter, or outline the text in Figma or Illustrator before editing.

**Files**
- `svg/`: vector masters. Use these where you can.
- `png/`: transparent PNGs. The number is the height in pixels.
- `logo-kit-preview.png` / `.html`: every version on its intended background.

**Versions**
- `logo-horizontal-color`: main logo on Cream or white.
- `logo-horizontal-reversed`: main logo on Navy or Deep Navy.
- `logo-horizontal-navy` / `-white`: one colour.
- `wordmark-navy` / `-cream`: the name only.
- `mark-primary`: app icon and avatar (Navy tile).
- `mark-reversed`: Cream tile, for dark backgrounds.
- `mark-blue`: accent tile.
- `glyph-*`: the TF symbol with no tile.

**Rules:** don't recolour, stretch, add effects, or put the colour logo on Royal Blue or on busy photos (use the white version). Give it clear space on every side at least the height of the "T" bar.
